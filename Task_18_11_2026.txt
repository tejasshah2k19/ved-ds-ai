Assignment 1: Employee Management System
Create the following DataFrame:
EmpID	Name	Department	Salary	Age
101	Rahul	IT	45000	25
102	Priya	HR	35000	28
103	Amit	IT	55000	30
104	Neha	Sales	25000	23
105	Raj	HR	60000	32
106	Kiran	IT	40000	26
107	Meena	Sales	50000	29
108	Jay	IT	30000	22

Tasks:
1. Find employees earning more than 40000.
2. Find employees working in IT.
3. Find employees whose age is below 25.
4. Find employees earning between 30000 and 50000.
5. Find employees from IT or HR.
6. Find employees who are not working in Sales.
7. Find employees whose salary is greater than 40000 and age is above 25.
8. Find employees earning less than 35000 or age greater than 30.
9. Display only employee names and salaries for employees earning above 45000.
10. Use query() to find employees with salary greater than 40000.

Assignment 3: Student Result Analysis
Create a DataFrame containing 10 students with the following columns:
- RollNo
- Name
- Course
- Python
- Java
- SQL
- City
Tasks:
1. Find students scoring more than 80 in Python.
2. Find students scoring below 40 in Java.
3. Find students who scored above 70 in all three subjects.
4. Find students from Ahmedabad or Surat.
5. Find students enrolled in Python or Java.
6. Find students whose SQL marks are between 50 and 80.
7. Find students whose Python marks are not equal to 100.
8. Find students older than 20 if you add an Age column.

10. Display only Name, Python and Java for students scoring above 80 in Python.
